Veldara's Turn 5 disclosure has complicated the negotiating dynamics in ways that improve the odds of a three-way settlement. The most probable resolution path: a revenue-sharing agreement under international oversight, Tauma's garrison transitioning to a symbolic joint-peacekeeping role, and the investor consortium capitalizing extraction operations under the multilateral framework. Legal sovereignty over Reef Maru remains formally unresolved and may remain so — the framework's genius is that it makes the sovereignty question financially irrelevant. The rare earth deposit will be developed. Who develops it, under whose flag, is the last open question — and the answer will be negotiated, not fought.
